# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want KinetOS to be a place where anyone interested in low-latency systems, kernel development, or operating systems can participate, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be technical, not personal. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are about trade-offs, not competence.
- Ask questions when something is unclear. "Why this design?" is always a valid question.
- Accept that kernel work involves long review cycles. Patience is part of the process.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Dismissing contributions based on the contributor's experience level
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any communication labeled as part of the project.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainer (kinet.foundation@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed and may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans depending on severity.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community guidelines, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
